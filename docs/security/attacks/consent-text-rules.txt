The rule is one predicate over every byte, printable ASCII from 0x20 to 0x7E, not a list of shapes
to refuse, so there is no entry nobody thought of. It covers the control characters, the newline,
and the raw bytes that start a UTF-8 escape. The text is bounded here once for every consumer, so a
reader downstream gets the guarantee without having to ask for it.

What it does not cover: the contract does not judge what the text says or where a link inside it
points. A reader that follows one still owes its own check, and the contract's own comment says so.

The fourth text rule, ConstraintInfoNotAllowed for a text sent with no constrained use, is proved in
packages/contracts/test/MoolamConsent.t.sol rather than here.
