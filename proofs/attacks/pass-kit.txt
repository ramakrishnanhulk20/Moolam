MOOLAM ATTACK PASS: THE PLATFORM KIT, THE MCP SERVER AND DREAMLOOM (WO-254)
============================================================================

What this is: every invariant the platform kit (P) and the MCP server (M) promise, and
Dreamloom's D1 to D7, attacked from outside against the deployed services. Invariants are in
docs/security/threat-model.md and kitchen/research/platform-kit-threat-model.md (P1 to P25,
M1 to M6) and PLAN.md 9.3 (D1 to D7). Each attack prints what was sent, with keys, handles and
session values shown as placeholders, and the answer's status and error word.
